## Payment

Bank transfer is what most customers use. Account details are sent with
the invoice — always check the account name reads **Aisha's Fashion
House** before you send anything. We will never ask you to pay a
different name, and we will never call to say our account has changed.

We also take cash in the shop and POS (card) in the shop.

We do not take payment on delivery for orders above ₦50,000.

Half payment holds an item for 48 hours. After that it goes back on the
shelf.

## Returns and exchanges

Fabric that has been **cut** cannot be returned. This is the one we get
asked about most and we cannot bend it — once a bundle is cut to your
length it cannot be sold to anyone else.

Uncut fabric, in its original wrap, can be exchanged within 5 days with
the receipt.

Ready-made clothing can be exchanged within 7 days if it has not been
worn, washed or altered. Tags must still be on.

If something arrives damaged or we sent the wrong item, tell us within
48 hours and we replace it and cover the delivery both ways. Send a
photo — it settles it immediately.

We do not give cash refunds. Exchange or store credit only.

## Sizing

Our ready-made clothing runs true to Nigerian standard sizing.

Small fits UK 8–10, Medium fits UK 12–14, Large fits UK 16–18, XL fits
UK 20–22.

If you are between sizes, take the larger one — our tailor can take a
garment in, but letting it out is limited by the seam allowance.

Send us your bust, waist and hip measurements and we will tell you which
size to take. We would rather do that than handle an exchange.

## Tailoring

We tailor in-house.

Standard turnaround is 5 to 7 working days from the day we take your
measurements.

Express (48 hours) costs ₦25,000 on top and depends on the week — ask
before you plan around it.

Bring your own fabric or use ours. Bringing your own does not reduce the
tailoring price.

For a wedding party, come to us at least three weeks ahead. Two weeks is
possible but we will charge express on some pieces.

## Aso-ebi and bulk orders

Minimum for aso-ebi pricing is 10 bundles.

10–19 bundles: 5% off.
20–49 bundles: 10% off.
50 or more: 15% off, and we deliver free anywhere in Lagos.

Bulk orders need 50% deposit to start. We hold the balance until
delivery.

Dye lots vary slightly between batches, so order the full quantity at
once. If you come back for two more bundles a month later we cannot
promise an exact match.

## Fabric care

Ankara — wash cold, inside out, separately for the first three washes.
The colour will run at first; this is normal for wax print and settles.

Lace — hand wash or dry clean. Do not wring. Dry flat in shade, not in
direct sun.

Aso-oke — dry clean only.

Iron everything on the reverse. A hot iron directly on lace or sequins
will melt them and it cannot be repaired.
